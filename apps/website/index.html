Why Jen.js?

TypeScript-First

Built for TypeScript. Strong type safety and excellent IDE support out of the box.

File-Based Routing

Routes are automatically generated from your file structure. Simple and intuitive.

Zero Config

Sensible defaults. Start building immediately. Configure only when you need to.

Preact-Powered

Lightweight component library. High performance. Compatible with React ecosystem.

Static & SSR

Generate static sites or server-render your pages. Choose based on your needs.

Minimal Output

Lean bundle sizes. Only ship what you need. Fast loading times guaranteed.
